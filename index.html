🎮 How to play
1. Choose a tower, then tap an empty grass area to place it.
2. Tap a tower on the map to select it. Use Upgrade to increase its power and range.
3. Press Start Wave to send enemies down the path. Towers attack automatically.
4. Earn coins for defeating enemies. If your lives reach zero, the game ends.
Tip: Place towers near corners so they can attack enemies for longer.
