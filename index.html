🎮 How to play
1. Select a tower and tap empty grass to build it.
2. Tap a built tower, then press Upgrade or Sell.
3. Press Start Wave. Towers attack enemies automatically.
4. Defeating enemies gives coins. Every fifth wave includes a boss!
Tip: Build near path corners. Upgraded towers deal more damage and have greater range.
